Checkbox — a hairline frame that fills solid ink when checked. No checkmark glyph: the fill IS the state (attendance-square precedent).

```jsx
<Checkbox label="Email me schedule changes" defaultChecked />
<Checkbox consent label="I have read this document and agree on behalf of Maya R." error="consent is required to continue" />
```

Use `consent` for waiver signing. Never restyle as a circle or switch.
